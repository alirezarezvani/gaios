# Direction: Enterprise sales leader

> **Direction, not data.** This shows the *shape* of a filled gAIOS for a sales leader. Write your
> own via `/setup`. Copy the thinking, not the words.

**Closest if:** you carry a number — running a pipeline, a team, or both, in B2B sales.

## One paragraph about you · `CLAUDE.md` intro + `context/about-me.md`
*Direction:* your remit, segment, and the number you own.
> *Illustrative:* "Enterprise Sales Director. I own a team of N AEs, the mid-market+ pipeline, and
> a quarterly quota. Accountable for forecast accuracy and net-new logos."

## Knowledge base · `CLAUDE.md` + `context/about-business.md`
*Direction:* what you sell, to whom, the motion, the typical deal shape.
> *Illustrative:* "We sell <product> to <ICP>; land-and-expand motion, ~$X ACV, 60–90 day cycles,
> multi-threaded buying committees."

## What eats your week / deepest pain · `context/about-me.md`
*Direction:* the selling-adjacent admin and the structure that's missing.
> *Illustrative:* "Call follow-ups, CRM hygiene, deal reviews, and rewriting prospecting emails.
> Deepest pain: prep — I walk into calls under-briefed on the account."

## Priorities — next ~90 days · `context/priorities.md`
*Direction:* dated, number-tied outcomes.
> *Illustrative:* "1) Close the quarter at >100% of quota. 2) Build 3x pipeline coverage for next
> Q. 3) Tighten the discovery-to-demo handoff."

## Guardrails — your sensitive-data line · `CLAUDE.md`
*Direction:* sales touches prospect PII and deal terms.
> *Illustrative:* "No prospect PII, account lists, or deal/pricing terms in the repo — pull live
> from CRM. **Draft** all external prospect/customer comms; I send. Internal deal notes may go in
> my voice."

## Voice fingerprint · `references/voice.md`
*Direction:* paste real emails; tone wins replies.
> *Illustrative:* "Short, value-first, one clear CTA. No fluff openers. Personalized first line."

## Likely connections · `connections.md`
*Direction:* the revenue stack.
> *Illustrative:* CRM (Salesforce) · email/calendar (Outlook/Google) · call recording/transcription ·
> sales engagement (Outreach) · docs (Drive). CRM is the source of truth — keep its data out of git.

## First prompts to try
- "Prep me for my 2pm with <account>: history, open items, who's in the room, my goal."
- "Debrief this call transcript into next steps and a CRM-ready summary."
- "Draft a follow-up email in my voice with one clear CTA."
