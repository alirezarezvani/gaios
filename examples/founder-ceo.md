# Direction: Founder / CEO — early-stage startup

> **Direction, not data.** This shows the *shape* of a filled gAIOS for a founder so blank
> `{{ }}` fields make sense. Write your own via `/setup`. Copy the thinking, not the words.

**Closest if:** you run a young company, wear every hat, and live between fundraising,
product, and go-to-market.

## One paragraph about you · `CLAUDE.md` intro + `context/about-me.md`
*Direction:* state your role, what you personally own *right now*, and the single outcome you're
accountable for this stage.
> *Illustrative:* "Founder/CEO of an early-stage B2B SaaS. I own fundraising, GTM, and the first
> 10 hires. Accountable for getting to a repeatable sales motion before runway ends."

## Knowledge base · `CLAUDE.md` + `context/about-business.md`
*Direction:* what the product does in one line, who it's for, the business model, the stage.
> *Illustrative:* "We sell <one-line product> to <ICP>. Seed stage, ~N customers, pre-Series A.
> Constraint: small team, long runway pressure."

## What eats your week / deepest pain · `context/about-me.md`
*Direction:* the resented hours and the missing structure the AIOS should fix first.
> *Illustrative:* "Investor updates, recruiting threads, and turning messy calls into follow-ups.
> Deepest pain: nothing is tracked — commitments fall through the cracks."

## Priorities — next ~90 days · `context/priorities.md`
*Direction:* 2–3 dated, "quarter is wasted if I miss these" outcomes.
> *Illustrative:* "1) Close the round by <date>. 2) Ship <flagship feature>. 3) Hire a founding AE."

## Guardrails — your sensitive-data line · `CLAUDE.md`
*Direction:* founders' riskiest data is financials and investor comms.
> *Illustrative:* "Never put cap table, runway, or live revenue figures in the repo — reference,
> don't transcribe. **Draft** investor/board comms; I send them. Internal team comms may go in my voice."

## Voice fingerprint · `references/voice.md`
*Direction:* how you actually write — paste real samples, don't invent.
> *Illustrative:* "Direct, optimistic-but-honest, short. Opens with the ask. No corporate filler."

## Likely connections · `connections.md`
*Direction:* what a founder usually wires first.
> *Illustrative:* CRM (HubSpot) · email/calendar (Google) · docs (Notion/Drive) · cap table (read-only) · Slack.

## First prompts to try
- "Draft this week's investor update from these bullets."
- "Turn this call transcript into owners + next steps."
- "What should I focus on this week given my priorities?"
