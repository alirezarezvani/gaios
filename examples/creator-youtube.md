# Direction: Solo creator / YouTuber

> **Direction, not data.** This shows the *shape* of a filled gAIOS for a creator business. Write
> your own via `/setup`. Copy the thinking, not the words.

**Closest if:** you're a one-person (or tiny-team) media business — content, audience, sponsors.

## One paragraph about you · `CLAUDE.md` intro + `context/about-me.md`
*Direction:* what you make, your channel/niche, and the outcome you're chasing.
> *Illustrative:* "Solo creator running a <niche> YouTube channel (~N subs). I own ideation,
> scripting, editing direction, and sponsorships. Accountable for consistent output and revenue."

## Knowledge base · `CLAUDE.md` + `context/about-business.md`
*Direction:* the audience, the content pillars, the revenue lines.
> *Illustrative:* "Audience: <who>. Pillars: <3 content themes>. Revenue: AdSense + sponsorships +
> a digital product. The channel *is* the business."

## What eats your week / deepest pain · `context/about-me.md`
*Direction:* the repeatable production drag.
> *Illustrative:* "Turning ideas into scripts, writing descriptions/titles, and sponsor outreach.
> Deepest pain: the idea backlog is chaos and nothing is repurposed across platforms."

## Priorities — next ~90 days · `context/priorities.md`
*Direction:* dated, measurable creator outcomes.
> *Illustrative:* "1) Ship 12 videos. 2) Land 3 sponsor deals. 3) Launch the email list."

## Guardrails — your sensitive-data line · `CLAUDE.md`
*Direction:* creators' risks are brand voice and disclosure compliance.
> *Illustrative:* "Never publish as me without showing a draft — voice matters. Always include
> required **sponsorship/ad disclosures**. No unverified claims in scripts — cite or flag. Keep
> sponsor contract terms out of the repo."

## Voice fingerprint · `references/voice.md`
*Direction:* paste real scripts/captions; your voice is the product.
> *Illustrative:* "Punchy, conversational, hook-first. Short sentences. Signature sign-off."

## Likely connections · `connections.md`
*Direction:* the creator stack.
> *Illustrative:* YouTube (analytics/Data API) · email (Gmail) · scheduling (calendar) · scripts/docs
> (Drive/Notion) · sponsor CRM (lightweight) · social schedulers.

## First prompts to try
- "Turn this rough idea into a title + hook + outline."
- "Repurpose this video script into a thread, a short, and a newsletter."
- "Draft a sponsor outreach email in my voice — flag where the disclosure goes."
