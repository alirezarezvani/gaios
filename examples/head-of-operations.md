# Direction: Head of Operations — SaaS scale-up

> **Direction, not data.** This shows the *shape* of a filled gAIOS for an ops leader. Write your
> own via `/setup`. Copy the thinking, not the words.

**Closest if:** you keep a growing company running — process, vendors, cross-team execution, the
operating cadence.

## One paragraph about you · `CLAUDE.md` intro + `context/about-me.md`
*Direction:* the functions you own and the leadership rhythm you keep.
> *Illustrative:* "Head of Operations at a ~150-person B2B SaaS. I own RevOps, vendor/contract
> management, internal tooling, and the weekly leadership cadence. Accountable for execution
> across teams."

## Knowledge base · `CLAUDE.md` + `context/about-business.md`
*Direction:* the company in a line, the operating model, the cross-functional surface you sit across.
> *Illustrative:* "Series B SaaS, <ICP>. I sit across Sales, CS, Finance, and Eng — I'm the glue
> that turns strategy into who-does-what-by-when."

## What eats your week / deepest pain · `context/about-me.md`
*Direction:* the coordination tax and the structure that's missing.
> *Illustrative:* "Status-chasing across teams, vendor renewals, and rewriting the same updates.
> Deepest pain: open loops live in my head and Slack, not anywhere trackable."

## Priorities — next ~90 days · `context/priorities.md`
*Direction:* dated operational outcomes tied to a metric.
> *Illustrative:* "1) Roll out the new QBR process. 2) Cut tool spend 15% at renewal. 3) Stand up
> a cross-team open-loops tracker."

## Guardrails — your sensitive-data line · `CLAUDE.md`
*Direction:* ops touches employee, customer, and vendor data — name the line and the regime.
> *Illustrative:* "GDPR + SOC 2. No employee PII, customer lists, or signed contract terms in the
> repo — reference, pull live. Internal comms may go in my voice; external/vendor comms are drafts."

## Voice fingerprint · `references/voice.md`
*Direction:* paste real samples.
> *Illustrative:* "Crisp, action-first, bulleted. Always ends with owner + due date. Warm but brief."

## Likely connections · `connections.md`
*Direction:* the ops stack to wire first.
> *Illustrative:* CRM (Salesforce) · tasks (Linear/Asana) · docs (Confluence/Notion) · calendar
> (Google/Outlook) · meeting notes (transcription) · finance (read-only).

## First prompts to try
- "Give me today's brief: priorities, open loops, and what's slipping."
- "Triage this dump of Slack asks into owners and next steps."
- "Draft the weekly ops update from these notes."
